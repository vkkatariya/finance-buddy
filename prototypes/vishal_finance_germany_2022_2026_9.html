Electronics
The 3-year rule
Never upgrade a device that still works. iPhone XR → 12 Pro after 10 months, Apple Watch Ultra on a deficit, Xiaomi Tablet + Smart Pen as a 3rd device. One laptop, one phone, one pair of earbuds at any given time.
✓ Saves ~€1,474 — biggest single lever
🍕
Food & Coffee
Cook 5 days, eat out 2
269 food-out entries averaging €5.67 each. Weekly Dosa.com visits (€20–27), frequent pizza (€11–12), daily coffee habit. Set a hard €15/mo cap — that's one proper meal out per week.
✓ Saves ~€748 over active months
👗
Clothing
One seasonal buy, one store
8 clothing transactions in May 2023 alone. Zara dependency ran hot through 2022–2023. Plan one buy per season (€80 max), stick to one store per season, use AliExpress for basics only.
✓ Saves ~€534 over the period
💻
Homelab
Defer passion projects
€491 on Radxa, Dell Optiplex, SSD, networking — all bought while €12k in deficit. These are exciting projects. Build them after hitting €0 net worth. Set a homelab budget in your savings plan.
✓ Saves ~€491 — free up deficit recovery
🎥
Entertainment
Set a €20/month envelope
“FUN” entries ranging €30–60 each are the culprit (€389 total). Cinema is fine (€59 over 3.5 years = €1.4/mo). Cash-only entertainment budget: take €20 out, when it’s gone it’s gone.
